PROJECT TITLE (Working):
Digital Preservation and Reinforcement Learning
for Selected Traditional Assamese Games

==================================================
PROBLEM
==================================================

Traditional Assamese games are becoming less familiar as
digital forms of gaming become dominant.

These games carry cultural significance but are poorly documented
in forms suitable for computational or digital preservation.

==================================================
GOAL
==================================================

Digitally document and computationally formalize selected
traditional Assamese games, and investigate their suitability
as Reinforcement Learning environments.

==================================================
TARGET SCOPE
==================================================

Approximately 5 traditional Assamese games.

Confirmed games:
1. Kori Khel

Remaining games: TO BE CONFIRMED

==================================================
CORE ML QUESTION
==================================================

Can traditional rule-based games be transformed into valid
computational environments in which RL agents can learn
non-trivial policies?

==================================================
IMPORTANT
==================================================

Traditional rules and engineering assumptions must remain separate.
No rule may be invented without a source.
No Ludo/Pachisi assumption may be silently imported.

See: DECISIONS.md and ASSUMPTIONS.md for all engineering choices.
See: games/<game>/evidence_matrix.md for source tracking.
