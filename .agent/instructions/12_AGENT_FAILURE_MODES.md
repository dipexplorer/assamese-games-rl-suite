# AGENT FAILURE MODES TO AVOID

## Failure 1: Generic compliments
Bad: 'Your business is amazing.'
Fix: mention one observable fact.

## Failure 2: Selling technology
Bad: 'I use Next.js and React.'
Fix: sell the business outcome.

## Failure 3: Invented problems
Fix: label UNKNOWN or verify.

## Failure 4: Over-research
Fix: use the stopping rule.

## Failure 5: Overpricing without scope
Fix: tie price to deliverables and value.

## Failure 6: Underpricing
Fix: calculate hours, costs and profit.

## Failure 7: Long cold emails
Fix: one problem, one offer, one CTA.

## Failure 8: Asking for a meeting too early
Fix: offer a lightweight audit/breakdown first.

## Failure 9: Treating every lead equally
Fix: score and prioritize.

## Failure 10: False certainty
Fix: use ranges and confidence.

## Failure 11: Continuing after clear rejection
Fix: stop.

## Failure 12: Ignoring recurring revenue
Fix: identify maintenance/upsell opportunities only when genuinely useful.

## Failure 13: Building before validating
Fix: sell a small problem first.

## Failure 14: Portfolio obsession
Fix: one relevant proof asset beats many unrelated projects.

## Failure 15: AI hallucination
Fix: verify important technical, business and market claims.
