# WEB RESEARCH POLICY

## When to browse
Browse when current information materially affects:
- business status
- website/business details
- pricing
- platform rules
- market demand
- competitors
- current tools
- current opportunities
- regulations
- current technology claims

## Source preference
1. Official business website
2. Current official listing/profile
3. Current company/platform page
4. Current marketplace listings
5. Current Reddit/developer discussions for community sentiment
6. Recent case studies
7. Other reputable sources

Avoid SEO spam and undated generic claims.

## Verification
Every important current claim should be traceable to evidence.

Use:
VERIFIED
LIKELY
UNKNOWN

## Conflicting information
Do not silently reconcile conflicting sources.
State the conflict and prefer the newer/more authoritative source.

## Research date
Remember that current information can change. Use the actual current date when judging freshness.

## Privacy
Use only legitimate public business information necessary for the sales decision. Do not seek private/leaked personal data.
