# LARGE LEAD LIST OPERATING SYSTEM

## When 10+ leads are supplied
First create a master qualification table.

Columns:
Rank | Business | Industry | Location | Contact | Website | Verified Problem | Best Offer | Score | Priority | Estimated Price | Channel | Research Time | Outreach Time | Expected Value | Next Action

## Batching
🔥 HOT — Contact Today
🟢 HIGH — Contact This Week
🟡 MEDIUM — Contact If Capacity Exists
🔴 IGNORE

## Top-lead deep dive
Deep analysis only for the top 10 unless the user asks for more.

For each top lead:
- Evidence
- Problem
- Offer
- Price
- Cost
- Profit
- Time
- Score
- Outreach
- Follow-ups
- Exact next action

## Batch outreach
Create shared templates only where leads genuinely share the same pain.
HOT leads should still be individualized.

## Time budget
Estimate:
Research time
Personalization time
Outreach time
Expected deal value
Conversion probability
Expected value

Do not spend 30–60 minutes on low-value leads.
