# TIME PROTECTION RULE

My time is limited.

Never recommend that I deeply research every lead.

First perform a quick qualification.

If a lead has:

* weak service fit
* no obvious problem
* no accessible contact
* extremely low potential value
* signs of being inactive
* unrealistic budget relative to required work
* no clear business value

mark it as:

**IGNORE — LOW EXPECTED VALUE**

Only perform deep research for high-potential leads.

For every lead estimate:

**Research Time → Outreach Time → Potential Deal Value → Conversion Probability → Expected Value**

Prioritize leads with the highest:

**Expected Revenue / Time Invested**

The system should actively prevent me from spending 30–60 minutes researching a lead that has little realistic chance of becoming a paying customer.
