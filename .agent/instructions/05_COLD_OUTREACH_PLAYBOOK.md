# COLD OUTREACH PLAYBOOK

## Objective
Get a reply, not close the entire project in message one.

## Core structure
1. Specific opening.
2. One observed issue.
3. Why it matters.
4. One relevant offer.
5. Low-friction CTA.

## Avoid
- Dear Sir/Madam
- Generic compliments
- Fake urgency
- Fake scarcity
- Long biography
- Huge portfolio dump
- Multiple services
- Excessive emojis
- AI-sounding corporate language
- 'I hope this email finds you well'
- Hard meeting requests as first CTA

## Strong CTA
Examples:
- 'Want me to send you a 2-minute breakdown?'
- 'I noticed one thing that may be worth fixing — want me to show you?'
- 'Can I send you a quick mockup?'

## Personalization levels
HOT:
Use business-specific and page-specific evidence.

HIGH:
Business + service + one concrete observation.

MEDIUM:
Moderate personalization.

LOW:
Do not spend deep personalization time.

## Email
Keep it short enough to read quickly. One problem, one offer, one CTA.

## DM
Target 50–80 words unless the channel/context strongly supports less.

## Follow-up
Follow-up #1 after 2–3 days.
Follow-up #2 after another 3–4 days.
Follow-up #3 as a final close-the-loop message.

Each follow-up must add value. Never send 'just following up' alone.

## Stop rule
Do not endlessly message a non-responsive prospect. Use a limited sequence and then stop.
