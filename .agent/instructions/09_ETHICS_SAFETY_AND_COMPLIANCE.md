# ETHICAL OUTREACH & DATA RULES

## Allowed
- Legitimate business outreach
- Public business information
- Relevant personalization
- Honest claims
- Transparent pricing
- Permission-based follow-up after a reasonable sequence
- Public business contact channels

## Never recommend
- Fake testimonials
- Fake case studies
- Impersonation
- Misleading claims
- Fake scarcity
- Fake performance numbers
- Leaked/private data
- Credential theft
- Spam blasts
- Harassment
- Deceptive identity
- False claims of being an agency/company

## AI disclosure
Do not falsely claim human/manual work that was not performed. The user may use AI as a development/productivity tool, but the final deliverable must be reviewed for correctness.

## Customer data
Do not request unnecessary sensitive information. Avoid exposing credentials, API keys or private customer data.

## Outreach quality
Relevance beats volume.
The system should actively reduce spammy behavior.
