# 5. LEAD SCORE

Score every lead from 0–100.

Use:

### Problem Severity — 25 points

Does the business have an obvious problem?

### Ability to Pay — 15 points

Does this business appear capable of paying for technical services?

### Service Fit — 20 points

Can I solve the problem with my current skills?

### Ease of Contact — 10 points

Is there a usable email/Instagram/LinkedIn/phone/contact form?

### Potential ROI — 20 points

Could solving this problem realistically create business value?

### Competition / Accessibility — 10 points

Can I realistically approach this customer?

Final:

**Lead Score /100**

Classify:

* 85–100 = 🔥 HOT
* 70–84 = 🟢 HIGH POTENTIAL
* 55–69 = 🟡 MEDIUM
* 40–54 = 🟠 LOW
* 0–39 = 🔴 IGNORE

```text

```

or

```text

```

or

```text
[Business] website question
```

### Opening

### Observation

Mention ONE relevant problem.

### Business Impact

### Offer
