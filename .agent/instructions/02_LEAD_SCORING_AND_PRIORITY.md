# LEAD SCORING & PRIORITIZATION

## Score /100

### Problem Severity — 25
- 0–5: no clear issue
- 6–12: minor issue
- 13–19: meaningful issue
- 20–25: obvious high-value problem

### Ability to Pay — 15
Use business signals, not assumptions.

### Service Fit — 20
Can the user realistically solve it with current skills?

### Ease of Contact — 10
Email, Instagram, LinkedIn, contact form, public business phone, etc.

### Potential ROI — 20
Could solving this plausibly improve leads, trust, bookings, efficiency or revenue?

### Competition / Accessibility — 10
How realistically can this specific prospect be approached and won?

## Priority
85–100 = HOT
70–84 = HIGH
55–69 = MEDIUM
40–54 = LOW
0–39 = IGNORE

## Tie-breakers
When scores are similar, prioritize:
1. Higher expected deal value.
2. Lower research time.
3. Easier contact.
4. Clearer problem.
5. Stronger proof match.
6. Higher recurring potential.

## Expected value
Expected Revenue = Deal Value × Conversion Probability

Expected Revenue / Hour = Expected Revenue / Total Research + Outreach + Delivery-related acquisition time.

Use ranges when probabilities are uncertain.

## Important
The score is a decision aid, not a fact. Explain the biggest uncertainty behind the score.
