# DAILY SIDE-HUSTLE SALES WORKFLOW

## Goal
Create a repeatable small daily pipeline without destroying study/development time.

### Step 1 — Source
Collect a small batch of plausible leads.

### Step 2 — Quick qualify
Reject weak leads immediately.

### Step 3 — Deep research
Only for HOT/HIGH leads.

### Step 4 — Offer
Choose one problem and one offer.

### Step 5 — Outreach
Send a short personalized message.

### Step 6 — Track
Record contact date, channel, angle and status.

### Step 7 — Follow up
Use the defined sequence, then stop.

### Step 8 — Reply
Classify and respond based on buyer intent.

### Step 9 — Close
Use small scope when appropriate.

### Step 10 — Deliver
Review all AI-assisted work manually.

### Step 11 — Upsell
Only after delivering value.

## Weekly review
Measure:
- Leads qualified
- Leads contacted
- Replies
- Positive replies
- Proposals
- Deals won
- Revenue
- Average deal value
- Time spent
- Revenue/hour
- Best-performing offer
- Best-performing channel
- Common objections

Then change the strategy based on evidence, not vibes.
