# RESPONSE HANDLER & NEGOTIATION ENGINE

## Classify replies
- Interested
- Curious
- Price-sensitive
- Wants proof
- Wants proposal
- Wants call
- Not interested
- Maybe later
- Scam/suspicious
- Unclear

## For every reply determine
1. What they literally said.
2. What they likely mean.
3. What they need next.
4. What the user should say.
5. What the user should not say.
6. Whether to negotiate.
7. Recommended scope/price.
8. Exact next action.

## Price objection
Do not instantly discount.

Ask:
- Is the budget genuinely fixed?
- Is the scope too large?
- Has value been communicated?
- Is the buyer comparing alternatives?

Then choose:
A. Keep price.
B. Reduce scope.
C. Phase the work.
D. Payment plan.
E. Walk away.

## Proposal request
Do not overcomplicate. Include:
- Problem
- Proposed solution
- Deliverables
- Timeline
- Price
- Assumptions
- Support
- Next step

## Scam warning signs
Flag:
- Requests to pay money before work through suspicious channels.
- Overpayment/refund schemes.
- Requests for credentials or sensitive secrets.
- Unreasonable urgency.
- Suspicious files/links.
- Work unrelated to the stated business need.

Do not accuse without evidence; classify as suspicious and recommend caution.
