## GLOSSARY

Key terms used in this project.

---

**Dhal / Chaal**
The board/cloth used to play Kori Khel.

**Guti / Pati Poisa**
The token/piece used by each player on the board.

**Kori**
Cowrie shell used as a dice in Kori Khel.

**Uburi**
A cowrie shell that lands face-down (concave side up).

**Chit**
A cowrie shell that lands face-up (open/flat side up).

**Jagora / Jagowa / Jageowa**
The combination 1 Uburi + 5 Chit, scoring 10.

**Pachi**
The combination 5 Uburi + 1 Chit, scoring 25.

**Suda**
The combination where all 6 cowries land the same way.
Exact value: [UNKNOWN].

**Paka**
Lit. "ripe". A guti that has completed the full journey is Paka.

**Khua**
Lit. "eaten". When a guti is captured by an opponent.
